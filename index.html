Mir JAYLANY

Made to my standard.

For years I modified, searched for, and sometimes simply made the things I wanted to use myself. A shirt with the right proportions. A tie for the right occasion. A pen that feels correct in the hand. A cup worth reaching for every morning. Even a phone mount that finally works the way it should.

I couldn't find exactly what I wanted, so I made it. This is that collection.

The standard

Not more things. Better things.

These are not merchandise. Nothing here carries my name because it needs a label — it carries my name because I could not find an acceptable version and had to resolve one myself.

An object enters the collection only after it has been made, used, and lived with long enough to know it holds up. Handmade pieces are issued in numbered editions and marked underneath.

The collection will grow slowly. That is the point of it.
